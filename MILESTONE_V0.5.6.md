# Msingi Accounting v0.5.6 — Layout: dropdown menu, fixed shell, Settings cards side by side

- **Left menu**: each group (Workspace, General ledger, Receivables & payables, Management) is a dropdown. Click a group name to open or close it. The group holding the current page stays open. Your open/closed choices are remembered on this device.
- **Scrolling**: the sidebar and page header stay fixed; only the content area under the header scrolls (with its own scrollbar). The whole window no longer scrolls.
- **Settings**: cards sit side by side (two columns on wide windows, one column on narrow ones). Tax rates are split into separate cards: Tax rates/VAT, Payroll taxes, Income tax & levy, Withholding tax.
- No accounting, VAT, payroll or data changes. No schema change.
